# Felipe Sembay

> AI is not just changing technology. It's reshaping the power dynamics between companies, professionals, and markets.

I'm a Data Scientist and ML Engineer with a background in **Economics**, passionate about understanding how technology, data, and artificial intelligence impact business, productivity, and human behavior.

Throughout my career, I've built solutions using Python, Machine Learning, Automation, Data Engineering, Web Scraping, LLM/RAG Systems, and data-driven applications across different business contexts.

But this space is not only about code.

Here, I write about artificial intelligence, data analysis, economics, tech careers, productivity, markets, and how all these transformations are redefining how companies compete and how professionals build value.

I believe we are entering an era where access to technology and the ability to adapt will become increasingly unequal competitive advantages. AI-powered companies will deliver more, faster, and with fewer people. Professionals who learn to work alongside technology will have a massive edge in the coming years.

And that's exactly the kind of discussion I enjoy bringing here.

Beyond articles and reflections, I also share real learnings from projects, studies, experiments, and systems I build involving Machine Learning, automation, data engineering, and applied AI.

I'm extremely analytical, competitive, and driven by continuous learning. I enjoy strategy games, reading, technology debates, and understanding how complex systems work — whether markets, algorithms, or human behavior.

If you're into technology, AI, data, and discussions about the future of work, business, and society, you'll probably enjoy what you find here.
