Customer Churn Prediction

This project presents a complete Machine Learning pipeline for binary classification, developed to predict customer churn in a telecommunications company. The work encompasses the entire lifecycle of a predictive model: data ingestion and cleaning, exploratory data analysis, feature engineering, model training, hyperparameter tuning, and interpretability analysis using SHAP.

The final LightGBM-based model achieved an AUC-ROC of 0.987 on the test set, demonstrating a high capacity to discriminate between customers prone to cancel and those who tend to stay. The risk analysis identified that the 20% of customers with the highest churn probability account for approximately 74.9% of cancellations, enabling the creation of highly targeted retention campaigns.

With this approach, the company can act proactively in customer retention, prioritizing resources on customers with the highest churn risk and maximizing the return on loyalty strategies.

Key Highlights

  • Handling of imbalanced data using resampling techniques and decision threshold optimization
  • Implementation of model explainability with SHAP, allowing the identification of factors that most influence churn
  • Experiment tracking and logging using MLflow, ensuring model reproducibility
  • Development of an interactive Streamlit application for results visualization and model interpretation
  • Construction of a complete Machine Learning pipeline, from preprocessing to final model evaluation
