Customer Churn Prediction

End-to-end binary classification pipeline to predict customer churn for a telecom company. The project covers data ingestion, exploratory data analysis, feature engineering, model training, hyperparameter tuning and explainability with SHAP values.

The final Gradient Boosting model achieved 87% AUC-ROC on the hold-out test set, enabling the business to proactively act on customers most likely to leave and reduce monthly churn by targeting retention campaigns.

Key Highlights

  • Imbalanced dataset handled with SMOTE + threshold tuning
  • SHAP explanations for individual predictions
  • Model exported with MLflow for reproducibility
  • Interactive results dashboard built in Streamlit
