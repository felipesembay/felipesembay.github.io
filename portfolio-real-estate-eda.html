Real-Estate Market EDA

Comprehensive exploratory analysis of the Brazilian real-estate market scraped from property listing platforms. The project maps price distribution across cities, identifies the strongest price drivers and builds interactive geospatial visualisations.

The dataset covers thousands of listings, cleaned and enriched with neighbourhood-level socioeconomic data to allow multi-dimensional analysis.

Key Highlights

  • Web scraping pipeline with automated data refresh
  • Interactive Folium map with price heatlayers
  • Multivariate outlier detection and treatment
  • Plotly Dash report for stakeholder review
