Brazil M2 Monetary Aggregate Forecasting

This project focuses on predicting the growth of the M2 monetary aggregate in the Brazilian economy. M2 is an indicator that measures the amount of currency "issued" and available in the economy (currency in circulation, demand deposits, and time deposits), serving as a crucial proxy used to anticipate inflation scenarios.

To produce these forecasts, macroeconomic time-series models were built using advanced techniques to extract trends and seasonalities from the data, isolating historical patterns and identifying global macroeconomic fluctuations.

Key Highlights

  • Time-series prediction algorithm development tailored to macroeconomic series modeling
  • Critical insights on Brazilian market liquidity over time as an inflation parameter
  • Extended confidence intervals visualization to represent forecasting uncertainty
