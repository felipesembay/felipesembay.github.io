Supply Chain Optimisation

Time-series forecasting system for demand planning across 50+ SKUs in a distribution company. Prophet models were trained per product family, capturing weekly seasonality, holidays and trend changepoints to generate 12-week forward forecasts.

The solution reduced stockout events by 18% and cut excess inventory costs by reducing over-ordering on slow-moving items.

Key Highlights

  • Multi-model architecture with automated retraining
  • MAPE < 12% across all SKU families
  • Confidence intervals for safety-stock planning
  • Streamlit dashboard for ops team self-service
